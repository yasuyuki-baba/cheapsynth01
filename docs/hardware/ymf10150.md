# Yamaha YM10150 (DTG): recorded background

YM10150 is recorded as the CS-01 family's digital tone generator. The
[GLS audit](Glissando-circuit-audit.md) preserves its timing branch connections.
Waveform/feet selection and keyboard/control roles are retained only as overview
findings; a full external pin-by-pin connection table is not preserved here.
No part-specific public datasheet is identified. Internal architecture claims
and their limits are recorded below. Evidence scope is summarized in
[the circuit evidence status](Circuit-evidence-status.md), with optional source
provenance in [the source catalog](Source-catalog.md).

## Shared-source waveform interpretation

The recorded interpretation is that a common pitch-generating clock/divider
source supplies pulse timing, from which the selectable waveforms are shaped.
This distinguishes waveform generation from the later VCF/VCA/EG changes that
create different instrument sounds. Exact divider ratios, shaping connections
and the allocation of shaping inside/outside YM10150 are not established here.
It does not prove that every hardware waveform is derived from one exposed
square-wave signal or follows the software's specific integration/ramp formulas.

The [software waveform-generation record](../software/Waveform-generation.md)
describes the implemented shared square/phase source and each transformation.
Those algorithms are software choices, not additional hardware evidence.

## Internal architecture: unverified interpretations

The earlier summary attributed the following interpretations to service material
and community discussion without exact page/thread identifiers.

| Subject | Recorded interpretation | Evidence limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Keyboard | Integrated key scanning and monophonic priority logic. | Internal logic, priority and retrigger rules are not established. |
| Pitch | Analog master clock with digital integer division or per-key tables. | Divider architecture, ratios and internal tables are not established. |
| Glissando | Clock/divider changes produce stepped pitch transitions. | The causal mechanism and GLS threshold/divider timing are not established; see [the glissando audit](Glissando-circuit-audit.md). |
| Waveforms | Pulse-derived triangle/saw shaping, internally or in surrounding circuitry. | The internal/external allocation and shaping topology are not established. |
| PWM/noise | LFO-driven pulse width and a separate noise source selected externally. | Detailed generation/routing is not independently verified here. |
| LFO | Integrated digital LFO with an analog output described as an LO pin. | Integration, generation method and pin attribution are not established; see [the LFO summary](lfo.md). |

External selection labels do not establish the chip's internal generation
method. Pinout, register layout, numerical pin counts, waveform timing and
thermal stability are not verified part specifications here. These interpretations
do not calibrate the software oscillator, LFO or keyboard handling; their current
implementation boundaries are recorded in
[circuit model unknowns](Circuit-model-unknowns.md).
